Azure Cost Calculator

Controller Tiers + Service Configuration | West US pricing | 1-Year Reservations available

How to Use
1️⃣
Select a Controller Tier
Pick Starter, Standard, Professional, or Enterprise based on how many access controllers your deployment manages (e.g. 50 / 500 / 2,000+).
2️⃣
Choose a Service Configuration
Minimum = cost-optimized dev/test SKUs. Standard = balanced production. Advanced = zone-redundant enterprise HA. Costs update instantly.
3️⃣
Adjust the Sliders
Tune heartbeat interval, credential events, and alarm events to match your expected traffic. These drive Cosmos DB and SQL Alarm cost calculations.
4️⃣
Toggle Reservations, Add-Ons & Support
Enable 1-Year Reservations for a ~35% discount on eligible services. Enable any Optional Add-Ons (geo-failover, backups, peak throughput). Select an Azure Support Plan to include it in the monthly total.
5️⃣
Review the Summary & Breakdown
The Summary shows monthly and annual totals. The Service Breakdown table itemizes every Azure service with its SKU and cost, and the bar chart shows spending by category.
6️⃣
Export or Print
Click Export to CSV to open in Excel, or Print for a formatted PDF. Use Reset to return all settings to the Standard defaults.
All prices are West US estimates based on the Azure Pricing Calculator (verified May 2025). Outbound egress bandwidth and the selected Azure Support Plan are included in all totals. Taxes, negotiated enterprise discounts, and region-specific pricing are not included. Always validate against the official Azure Pricing Calculator before committing to a budget.
